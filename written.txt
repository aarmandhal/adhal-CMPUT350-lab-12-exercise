Q3. What happens to the bounding boxes with update + fullRefit?

The tree's structure never changes, so as circles dirft apart the internal boxes stretch to keep covering the children that are now far from each other.
The boxes get bigger and overlap more over time which makes the tree less effective and it makes the traversal slower.

Q4. What happens with reset (rebuild every frame) instead?

Rebuilding every frame keeps the boxes tight with little overlap, so the boxes jump around as the grouping changes.
Each frame costs a full build instead of a cheap refit, but the tracing stays fast because the tree is always a good fit.

Q5. Why are some circles drawn with their sides cut off?

Circle centres are placed anywhere from 0 to the screen width/height, so circles near the border extend past the edge of the window and the part outside isn't drawn.
Circles that overlap will also hide each other, depending on which one the BVH visits last.
